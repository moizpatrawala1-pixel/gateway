Our Roots

Crafting Trust
Since Day One

Every great manufacturing legacy begins with a simple commitment to quality. Gateway Metal Industries was founded in 1975 by Ismail Sulemanji Patrawala in a modest workshop in Lower Parel, Mumbai's historic industrial heartland.

Built on the core belief that packaging is the ultimate reflection of a brand's care and durability, we spent our first three decades mastering the art of precision metal fabrication. Word of our tight tolerances and uncompromising quality spread, allowing us to grow alongside India's booming FMCG and industrial sectors.

In 2009, anticipating the need for massive volume scaling and modern automation, we shifted operations to a state-of-the-art facility in Taloja, Maharashtra. Today, we stand as a premier metal tin packaging supplier, bridging traditional craftsmanship with global supply chain capabilities.
